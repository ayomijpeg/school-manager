## 1. Introduction & Overview

This proposal outlines a plan to upgrade the **Yosola Schools** management portal with **Five (5) Practical, High-Impact Features**. 

These new features are designed to address the school's most important daily needs:
- Recovering outstanding school fees faster and more smoothly.
- Reaching parents directly on WhatsApp without extra messaging costs.
- Generating beautiful, complete terminal report cards with character ratings and next term dates.
- Saving time for teachers during morning attendance.
- Printing student ID cards in-house to save outside printing expenses.

This document also includes a **Monthly Developer Management Agreement** to ensure the school system remains fully operational, backed up, and supported at all times.

### Feature 1: Instant WhatsApp Parent Messages
*Reach parents where they actually look — zero extra SMS fees.*
- **One-Click Fee Reminders:** With a single click from any student's bill, the system prepares a friendly, professional WhatsApp message for the parent:
  > *"Dear Mr/Mrs [Parent Name], this is a gentle reminder from Yosola Schools regarding [Child Name]'s outstanding fee of ₦[Amount] for this term. Kindly make payment to [Bank Name], Account: [Account Number]. Thank you for your support."*
- **Morning Absence Notification:** If a child is marked absent in the morning, a message is immediately ready to confirm with the parent if the child is unwell or at home.
- **Report Card Release Notice:** Alert parents on WhatsApp the moment end-of-term results are published.

---

### Feature 2: Complete Terminal Report Cards
*Covers academics, character, behavior, and next term resumption details.*
- **Character & Behavior Ratings (Affective Domain):** Simple 1 to 5 star rating for important habits: *Punctuality, Neatness, Politeness, Honesty, Attentiveness, Sportsmanship, and Handwriting*.
- **Class Position & Averages:** Automatically shows each student's overall position (e.g., *1st, 2nd, 3rd in class*), total score, and the class average.
- **Continuous Assessment (CA) Breakdown:** Supports the standard Nigerian grading formula:
  - 1st Test / Assignment (20 marks)
  - Mid-Term Test / Project (20 marks)
  - Final Examination (60 marks)
  - Total = 100%
- **Printed Resumption & Fee Notice:** The bottom of every printed report card will clearly display:
  - *Vacation Date*
  - *Next Term Resumption Date*
  - *Next Term School Fees Due*
- **Smart Teacher & Principal Comments:** Ready-made comment suggestions so teachers do not have to write repetitive remarks from scratch.

---

### Feature 3: School Fee Recovery & Exam Clearance Passes
*Protect school revenue and ensure smooth examination periods.*
- **Fee Debtors List:** A clear list showing all students with unpaid fees, grouped by class, so the management always knows total outstanding fees at a glance.
- **Printable Exam Passes (Exam Dockets):** Before examinations, the school can print wallet-sized slips (6 per page) for cleared students:
  > **"YOSOLA SCHOOLS — CLEARED FOR EXAMINATIONS"**  
  Teachers and hall invigilators can quickly check these passes at the exam hall door.
- **Official Stamped Payment Receipts:** Instantly print clean payment receipts with the Yosola Schools crest, receipt number, and a green "PAID" stamp whenever a parent pays.

---

### Feature 4: Quick 30-Second Morning Attendance
*Simple and effortless for class teachers during morning roll call.*
- **One-Click "Mark All Present":** The teacher selects their class and clicks one button to mark all pupils present. They then simply tap the few pupils who are late or absent.
- **Morning Attendance Overview:** A clean daily summary on the principal's dashboard showing today's attendance rate and who is absent.

---

### Feature 5: In-House Student ID Card Printing & School Identity
*Save money on outside printing vendors and improve pupil safety.*
- **Printable Student ID Cards:** Generate 8 ready-to-print ID cards per sheet of paper or card stock. Each card features the Yosola Schools logo, pupil photo, admission number, class, blood group, and emergency contact number.
- **Morning Assembly Birthday Celebrants:** A helpful reminder card showing whose birthday it is this week so pupils can be celebrated and prayed for during morning assembly.
- **Authorized Pickup Contacts:** Clear records on each student's profile showing approved parents, guardians, or drivers permitted to pick up the child after school closing.
